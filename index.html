REMAINDER TRICK

Euler's Theorem

For any two numbers a and n that are co-prime, $a^{\phi(n)} \equiv 1 \pmod n$, where $\phi(n)$ is Euler's totient number of n. Perfect for high-power modular arithmetic problems!

ARITHMETIC SPEED HACK

Product Constancy & Ratios

If distance is constant, $S_1 \cdot T_1 = S_2 \cdot T_2$. Ratio of Speed is inversely proportional to Time. A speed increase of $1/x$ results in a travel time reduction of $1/(x+1)$. Avoid complex equations!

ALGEBRA MINDSET

Smart Substitutions

In equations with constraints (e.g., $x + y + z = 10$), choose friendly extreme values like $x=10, y=0, z=0$ or symmetric values $x=y=z=10/3$ to test options in seconds instead of doing lengthy algebraic proofs.
